THE PRACTICE / HOW THE WORK HOLDS

Good products have a strong foundation. And a human point of view.

I bring twenty years of design and engineering to the space between an ambitious idea and a working product. Less noise. More purpose. Enough structure to make the next decision clear.

Clarity is a structural decision. Like a crease in paper, a good decision made early decides where everything else can bend / and what refuses to. Built on intention, designed for people: that is the fold this practice keeps returning to.

Twenty years making complex products feel natural / and still curious about what happens next.
