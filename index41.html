.SYNOPSIS...

High pressure has been building over Portland since 1998. Products are designed at the seam where intent becomes interface, where a half-formed idea has to choose what kind of system it becomes. That is where the interesting decisions live: pacing, posture, what shows up first, what stays hidden, and what the model is allowed to assume.

AI made the design problem about judgment: what to build, how it should behave in someone’s actual life, and when to ship it. Expect the prototype, the build, and someone who stays for the part where it has to work for real users.
