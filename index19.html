Production notes

How the film was made.

Design direction

The work starts by finding a framing device strong enough to hold the content. A portfolio can be an atlas, a shell, a record, a terminal, or a title sequence if the metaphor clarifies the person.

Technical direction

Marco's value sits in the overlap: he can think in interaction systems, then make enough of the thing real that the team stops debating vapor.

AI direction

The interesting question is not "can AI make a page?" It is "can a designer use AI as a volatile creative material and still impose taste, structure, and standards?"

Enterprise direction

Checkout, wallet, cart, account, internal tooling, and commerce systems need restraint. The drama is in making the heavy thing feel inevitable.
