§ 001 the invocation

Hear the Subject Named in the manner of an opening prayer to a working life

aetatis suae XLV

Marco van Hylckama Vlieg, here invoked as the Magus, arcanum the twenty‑third, has spent twenty years setting type, drawing systems, shipping product, and now teaching machines to ship beside him. The Netherlands gave him a language, California gave him an industry, and Oregon — where the rain falls straight and the cedars are honest — gives him a desk.

He has been the designer of record at Google (Cloud AI · Assistant · Android prototyping), Yahoo! (director of UX prototyping), Walmart Labs (principal product designer), Endemol (UX & engineering lead), and now Kohl’s — senior product designer & generative‑AI specialist, leading UX across cart, checkout, wallet, account, and in‑store self‑checkout while driving AI tooling and education across the design org. He has been the designer of his own record, too: ai‑created.com, an independent product lab whose recent shipments — My AI Diary, WatchGPT, Human Actually, Calorie Counter, AI Usage, Color Clock, the Audio Visualizer Suite, and the arcade studies Vectronix and Radial Drift — appear in the minor arcana below. Each card opens the working product.

The craft is the same it has always been — pay attention, name what is wrong, fix it, and then fix it again, with feeling. What changed is the partner at the bench: a model, a context window, a working tool. He is fluent in that conversation. He refuses the average answer the model would otherwise give — not in spite of the tool but in full service of it.

§ 005 the oracle

The Oracle Speaks.

Each row below is a translation of one card from the spread above — what the card actually means about a working life. This is the bio you can use. A position‑by‑position breakdown of twenty years in product design, design engineering, and AI‑native work.

read by the deck itself, candidly
cast at the kitchen table, no incense
witnessed by the cat (mt. tabor)
divination · the ten positions
i· self
The Magus is not a king; he is a person at a workbench with one of each tool laid out. The subject is fluent in making things — design, code, words, voice — and is unromantic about which one it took to ship.
ii· crossing
The Tower is the work going through transformation in real time: AI dismantles the practice and rebuilds it in the same week. The crossing is not an obstacle — it is the new weather.
iii· root
The World beneath it all is the work record above: Endemol (UX & engineering), Google (Cloud AI · Assistant · Android prototyping), Yahoo! (director of UX prototyping), Walmart Labs (principal product designer), Kohl’s (current — senior product designer & generative‑AI specialist). The taste was not invented yesterday; it is the foundation that lets the new tools move quickly.
iv· past
The Fool is the Netherlands — a kid with a bag of nothing and a road in front of him, building multilingual web platforms at Endemol. The willingness to start without permission still rides in every project that follows.
v· crown
The Star overhead is the independent lab, ai‑created.com — a small north light, not a spotlight. My AI Diary, WatchGPT, Calorie Counter, Color Clock, Human Actually, AI Usage — all built end‑to‑end, all live, all in the minor arcana below.
vi· future
The Sun in front: an AI‑native practice run by a person with taste. WatchGPT on the wrist, My AI Diary in the pocket, Calorie Counter in the kitchen — shipped, not described. The subject does not fear being replaced; he is too busy replacing the boring parts of his own work.
vii· hand
The Maker — the personal disposition. The subject is allergic to template output; gives feedback in plain language; refuses the first vibe‑coded answer. Drives AI tooling & education across the design org at Kohl’s — comfortable as IC, lead, or director.
viii· house
The Wheel — the environment. Currently Portland, Oregon. Currently Kohl’s by day, the lab by night; both running in parallel, each feeding the other.
ix· vision
The Moon — the held image. Software that feels hand‑set, designed by a person, with the joints visible and no embarrassment about them. Color Clock for the love letter; Vectronix for the hardcore; Audio Visualizer Suite for the craft.
x· outcome
The Work Itself, which has no number because it is its own arcanum: ship work that is original, useful, and recognisably done by a person. The deck above is the receipt — click anything.
