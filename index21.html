Ma (間) is the Japanese idea that the space between things is itself a thing — that an interval is designed, not left over. Twenty years of product design, composed here almost entirely of what was left out.

一

Position

ichi · where one stands

I design products at the seam where intent becomes interface — where a half-formed idea has to choose what kind of system it wants to become.

AI didn't shrink the design problem. It made the problem about judgment — choosing what to build, how it should behave in someone's actual life, and which nine ideas to decline so the tenth can breathe.
