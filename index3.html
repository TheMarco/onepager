Dear hiring manager,

Thank you for opening this. I assume you came here looking for a senior designer, a design engineer, or somebody who can carry an AI‑native product from a half‑formed idea through to a thing that actually ships. I've been doing that work for twenty years, so I thought I’d write you directly. The portfolio version of all of this is over at ai-created.com, but a letter felt more honest.

My practice sits at the seam where strategy, design, interaction behavior, and the realities of implementation collide. I write the prototype, ship the build, and stay around for the slow, unglamorous part where the thing has to be good for real users. That part is most of the work. It’s also the part most portfolios skip, which is why I’d rather hand you the resume below than show you another deck.

a quick note, in pen:
AI didn’t shrink the design problem. It made the problem about judgment, not output. Worth pinning on a wall somewhere.

Twenty years has meant time at Google, Yahoo!, Walmart Labs, Kohl’s, and Endemol, plus an independent product lab that doesn’t sleep. I’ve led prototyping teams, mentored designers, shipped retail and AI features at enterprise scale, and built consumer apps end-to-end on my own. I’m comfortable as a senior IC, a lead, or a director — whichever shape your team actually needs. Here’s the short version, typed below:
