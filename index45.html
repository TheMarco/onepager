Kintsugi repairs broken pottery with lacquer mixed with gold. The break is not hidden. It is the most valuable part of the bowl, because it records where the bowl has been. Careers break in the same places: a company ends, a platform shifts, a craft has to be relearned. This bowl has five breaks. All of them hold.

The bowl mends itself. No two mendings are alike.

Five breaks, all holding.

Each row names a break: the moment one period ended and the next had to be joined to it. The gold line in each row is what the joining produced.

The repair method.

Western repair hides the seam. Kintsugi does the opposite, because the seam is information. A hiring manager does not need a career without breaks. Every long career has them. What matters is whether the joins are stronger than the original wall, and whether the person can show you the gold.

AI is the newest break, and the widest. Production skills that took twenty years to fire in the kiln suddenly needed rejoining to a model that drafts, codes and critiques. The mend on this bowl is still wet. It is also, so far, the strongest seam in the piece.
