Reading the column.

Most portfolios list jobs. A core sample shows something a list cannot: continuity. The engineering depth at the bottom is still load-bearing. The prototyping teams in the middle are why the prototypes at the top behave like real software. Nothing was discarded on the way up. Each period compressed the one below it and made it stronger.

The work sits at the seam where intent becomes interface, where a half-formed idea has to choose what kind of system it becomes. AI moved the hard part from production to judgment: what to build, how it should behave in a real life, and when to ship it. Twenty-eight years of strata is what that judgment is made of.
