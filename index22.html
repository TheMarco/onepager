I design products at the seam where intent becomes interface — where a half-formed idea has to choose what kind of system it wants to become.

AI did not shrink the design problem. It made the problem about judgment — what to build, how it should behave in someone's actual life, and shipping it before the moment passes.

The portfolio is the proof. Everything reported below exists, runs, and is used.
